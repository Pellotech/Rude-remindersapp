Good catch — don’t push yet. This screen is saying your AndroidManifest.xml has a real error (red) plus some warnings. A commit can still happen, but if the manifest is broken it can bite you later (and Replit will pull the broken state). Let’s fix the 1 red error first, then commit again.

What matters on that list
✅ The only thing we must fix right now:

Error: Permission exists without corresponding hardware <uses-feature ... camera ...> tag

This usually happens when you have a camera permission like:

android.permission.CAMERA

…but you don’t have (or Android thinks you don’t have) the matching:

<uses-feature android:name="android.hardware.camera" android:required="false" />

Also important
Warning: <uses-permission> appears after <application>

That’s not ideal. All <uses-permission> and <uses-feature> lines must be ABOVE <application> (directly under <manifest>).

So the “fix” is basically: move your permission/feature lines up to the top.

Do this exactly (safe template)

Open:
android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml

Near the top it should look like this order:

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="utf-8"?>
<manifest xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android"
    package="com.rudereminders.app">

    <!-- Permissions (must be ABOVE application) -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET" />
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.CAMERA" />

    <!-- Camera feature declaration (fixes your red error) -->
    <uses-feature
        android:name="android.hardware.camera"
        android:required="false" />

    <application
        ...
    >
        ...
    </application>
</manifest>

Key rules:

✅ <uses-permission> goes above <application>

✅ <uses-feature ... camera ...> goes above <application>

✅ android:required="false" means: “works even if device has no camera”

What about the “XML tag has empty body” warning?

That usually means you have a tag like:

<uses-feature ...></uses-feature>


but nothing inside. That’s fine, but Android Studio prefers the self-closing format:

✅ Use this instead:

<uses-feature android:name="android.hardware.camera" android:required="false" />
