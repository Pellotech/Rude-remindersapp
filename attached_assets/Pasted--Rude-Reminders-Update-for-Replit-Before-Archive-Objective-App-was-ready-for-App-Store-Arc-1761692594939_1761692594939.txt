2. 🧩 AppIcon Image (include in assets)

3. ⚙️ Ensure Info.plist includes

</array>

Add this flag in ios/App/App.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj under Other Warning Flags:

This silences the Cordova header warnings that break the build.

5. 🧩 Re-Sync and Build
npx cap sync ios
npx cap open ios
